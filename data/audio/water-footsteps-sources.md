# Shallow-water footsteps

The eight `footstep-shallow-water-*.ogg` clips are edited from
[Wading in Shallow Water.wav by ryansitz](https://freesound.org/people/ryansitz/sounds/342932/),
released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Source audio was taken from the public high-quality Freesound preview. Individual
steps were trimmed, high-pass filtered, faded, and encoded to 48 kHz mono Ogg Vorbis.
